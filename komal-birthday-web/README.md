# 🌸 Happy Birthday Komal! ✨

An interactive, high-fidelity digital celebration webpage made with love for **Komal**, inspired by `birthday.myheartcraft.com`.

---

## 🎂 Features & Scenes

1. **Scene 1: The Surprise Gate**
   - 3D wax-sealed pink envelope stamped with **"K"** and a glowing heart.
   - Tap to break the seal and start the ambient romantic music.
   
2. **Scene 2: Balloon Pop Extravaganza**
   - 6 floating pastel balloons that can be tapped to pop.
   - Realistic popping sound effects and colorful mini-confetti explosions.
   - Reveals 6 sweet, deeply personal compliments about Komal.

3. **Scene 3: Birthday Cake & Candle Ritual**
   - 2-tier pastel strawberry birthday cake customized with *"Happy Birthday Komal 💕"*.
   - Multi-layered glowing candle flames that flicker realistically.
   - **Blow detection**: She can blow gently into her tablet/device microphone or tap each flame to extinguish them!
   - Extinguishing triggers rising smoke puffs, golden sparklers, and celebratory fanfare.

4. **Scene 4: Love Letter & Polaroid Memory Gallery**
   - Two-column tablet layout:
     - Left: Romantic stationery love letter with personalized prose and elegant typography.
     - Right: 4 vintage polaroid cards with interactive tilt, paper clips, and captions.
   - **Instant Live Photo Uploader**: Click *"Drop Your Real Photos Here"* or the top-right ⚙️ icon to upload real photos of Komal directly from your computer or tablet.

5. **Scene 5: 3D Mystery Gift Box Finale**
   - Wrapped gift box with satin ribbon.
   - Tap to untie the ribbon — the 3D lid pops off with a grand shower of fireworks and confetti.
   - Displays a royal keepsake birthday card: *"Happy Birthday, My Queen Komal! 💖"*.

---

## 📱 Tablet Optimization ("Tab Version")
- **Touch-first design**: 44px+ touch targets with bubbly, responsive feedback.
- **Orientation Adaptive**:
  - **Tablet Landscape** (iPad, Android Tablet 16:10 / 4:3): Expansive dual-column layout.
  - **Tablet Portrait & Mobile**: Single-column vertical flow with gesture support.
- **Tablet Gestures**: Swipe left to advance and swipe right to go back.
- **Offline & Zero-dependency**: Works instantly in any browser without needing Node.js, Python, or npm installations.

## 🚀 How to Run & Share

### Quick Local Preview
- Double-click **`run_preview.bat`** (or open **`index.html`** in any browser).

### How to Share with Komal:
1. **GitHub Pages (Free & Instant)**:
   - Create a GitHub repository and push the contents of `komal-birthday-web/`.
   - Enable GitHub Pages under Settings > Pages. You will get a link like `https://username.github.io/komal-birthday/` to send to her via WhatsApp or Instagram!
2. **Netlify Drop / Vercel**:
   - Simply drag and drop the `komal-birthday-web` folder onto [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) for a free live URL in 10 seconds.
